Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard rule of thumb for 1099 vs W2?

A quick rule of thumb used by accountants is to divide your target W2 annual salary by 1,000 (e.g. $100,000 salary ➔ $100/hr 1099 rate) or add 40% to 50% to your W2 hourly equivalent ($50/hr W2 ➔ $70–$75/hr 1099). This covers self-employment tax, uncompensated downtime, healthcare, and accounting expenses.

Can an S-Corporation lower my 1099 taxes?

Yes. Once net freelance profits exceed $80,000 to $100,000, electing S-Corp taxation allows you to pay yourself a reasonable W2 salary and take the remaining profits as distributions, which are exempt from the 15.3% self-employment tax. This routinely saves contractors $5,000 to $12,000 annually.
